The professional story

Leadership grounded in experience. Innovation shaped by purpose.

Building meaningful businesses, stronger institutions and memorable experiences—while keeping people, place and sustainability at the centre.

A versatile leader and entrepreneur with over 40 years of experience across the hospitality, aviation and education sectors. Pallav's work spans scaling operations, restoring heritage properties and pioneering eco-friendly business models.

A Certified Hospitality Educator, he brings a track record in policy research, team building and strategic turnaround for competitive markets. His work connects commercial performance with cultural preservation, community participation and practical sustainability.

He is also a prolific storyteller, author and documentary producer, bringing a strong narrative perspective to tourism, culture and the human side of business.
